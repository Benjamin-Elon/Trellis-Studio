# Garden structures <!-- NEW -->

The garden module's **Add Structure** control creates a selected, named, layout-only vertex. Structures do not block planting allocation or irrigation. Creation needs an editable garden, but no bed settings. The starting rectangle is 2 m square (180 graph units), reduced proportionally for smaller gardens and positioned inside the garden at the click location. <!-- NEW -->

Each structure is a direct garden child, non-connectable, and cannot receive dropped children. Its editable name defaults to **Structure**, allows duplicates, and appears on the canvas. Enter and blur save the name; Escape restores the value from focus. The overlay also provides **Edit Footprint** and immediate, undoable **Remove Structure**. Focused name drafts survive overlay refreshes. The input isolates pointer, mouse, and keyboard events using the garden module's name-field pattern; ordinary input events retain their browser defaults for focus, caret movement, and selection. <!-- CHANGE -->

Selecting a structure shows its overlay. A plain primary click on the same sole selected structure hides it; another shows it. Manual dismissal survives view/model refreshes until selection changes. A press alone never toggles. Movement of four pixels or more remains a drag even after returning to the press point. Modified clicks, alternate buttons, second double-click presses, panel controls, handles, outside releases, pointer cancellation, window blur, and selection changes during the gesture never toggle. Shared manipulation suppression temporarily hides overlays without changing manual dismissal. Footprint editing keeps Done/Cancel visible and disables click toggling. Overlay positioning is unchanged. <!-- NEW -->

## Footprint editing <!-- NEW -->

The **View options ▾** dropdown beside zoom contains **Show harvest size**, **Show links**, and **Show structure side lengths**. Harvest-size and link controls retain their existing scopes. Side lengths apply to every rendered structure on the current page, including deselected structures, and default to hidden. Their device-local preference is keyed by user, diagram, and page; unidentified diagrams use root-isolated session preferences. Viewing options work in read-only diagrams and never edit XML or history. <!-- NEW -->

Each polygon segment, including the closing segment and separate collinear segments, receives its physical length using the owning garden's existing unit formatter. Labels follow edges, remain upright, ignore pointer input, and offset outward with bounded local overlap adjustments. Tight corners and neighbouring structures may still crowd labels. Ordinary labels follow manipulation suppression; footprint editing always displays live draft measurements, including handle previews, regardless of the checkbox. Done, Cancel, and history dismissal restore preference-controlled visibility. Labels are excluded from export and printing. <!-- NEW -->

**Edit Footprint** owns the shared `structure-footprint` interaction mode. Normal movement, resizing, rotation, deletion, and label editing are disabled for the structure while it is being edited. Corner handles move points; dragging a white midpoint inserts a point. Pressing Delete or Backspace removes the selected corner. Panning, zooming, and native grid snapping remain available. The dashed outline is a local preview; the persisted shape stays unchanged until **Done**. <!-- NEW -->

Outlines may be concave, but must have at least three distinct corners, nonzero area, and no crossings or overlapping edges. Curves and holes are unsupported. Invalid operations retain the last valid outline and show an explanation. Points may extend beyond the original rectangle, but their rotated footprint must remain inside the garden. Rebasing the saved bounding box preserves world positions, including rotated and flipped outlines. <!-- NEW -->

**Done** stores the entire footprint as one undoable transaction. An unchanged draft creates no edit. **Cancel**, Escape, selecting another object, and switching modes ask before discarding a changed draft. **Keep Editing** retains selection and draft; **Discard Changes** completes the requested transition. Concurrent exit attempts share one confirmation, and only the first accepted transition can replace the active owner. <!-- NEW -->

Undo and Redo use the existing transient-preview history controller. The first command closes an unchanged footprint editor immediately or asks before discarding changed points (including an in-progress handle preview). Keep Editing leaves all participating drafts and diagram history untouched. Approval closes the editor and consumes the command; a subsequent command advances diagram history. Both actions remain available with empty history stacks while an editor is active. Repeated commands during confirmation are consumed without queuing history changes. Owners unregister on close/destruction, and pending approval is invalidated if its registered owners are replaced. Existing participants without approval hooks still dismiss synchronously. <!-- NEW -->

Deletion, permission loss, or conflicting model changes disable Done and retain the draft with an explanation. Cancel remains available. Invalid stored footprint data is reported without rewriting it. Drafts are transient and are not saved across application shutdown. <!-- NEW -->

## Persistence and integration <!-- NEW -->

Rectangular structures and beds share the garden's enabled path-width presets, units, tolerance (25% of the preset, capped at 30 cm), and Alt bypass. Creation, pointer movement, and completed resizing can translate a rectangle to the selected clearance without changing its angle or requested dimensions. Keyboard nudges, cloning, reparenting, ordinary multiple selections, rotation, and footprint editing do not trigger snapping. Existing bed workspace handles still carry their contents using the final snapped movement. <!-- CHANGE -->

Axis-aligned rectangles retain directional gap bands and two-axis snapping. A pair involving an angled rectangle uses the shortest distance between its actual outlines, including corner-to-edge and corner-to-corner distances, and shows a measurement segment. The smallest valid correction wins against the combined axis-aligned correction; ties prefer a smaller path width and then stable object IDs. An angled correction satisfies one neighbour. Final previews and commits share the evaluator, and corrections that leave the garden or fail to produce the advertised clearance are rejected. <!-- CHANGE -->

Flips, right-angle rotations, and redundant straight-side footprint points are supported. Irregular or malformed footprints are excluded as both moving objects and targets. Touching or overlapping pairs do not produce clearance snaps; overlap otherwise remains allowed. Minimum clearance describes the narrowest point, not a uniform walking corridor, and competing targets may switch during movement. The graph-local `graph.__trellisPlantingGroup.pathSnap` service provides pure `evaluate(cell, optionalGeometry)`, transaction-free `applyNoTxn(cell)`, and native `previewResize(handler)` integration. No additional persisted settings or footprint data are introduced. <!-- CHANGE -->

The XML value stores `garden_structure="1"`, `label`, and `structure_footprint_json={"version":1,"points":[[x,y],...]}`. Coordinates are normalized to the native vertex bounds. The style contains a generated embedded Draw.io stencil; geometry and style retain native position, size, rotation, flip, and appearance. Copying, serialization, SVG rendering, and printing therefore use native mechanisms. <!-- NEW -->

`graph.__trellisStructures` exposes creation, footprint editing, draft corner operations, Done/Cancel, validation, and footprint inspection. Its mutations use native model transactions and permission checks. Structures are excluded from conversion to beds and participate in the existing unit-aware dimensions and graph-local overlay suppression. Native transforms cannot place their footprint outside the garden; garden resizing cannot shrink below its structures. Overlap between layout objects remains allowed. <!-- NEW -->

The shared interaction controller supports `requestAsync` and `closeActiveAsync` with an optional `beforeClose` hook. A rejected guard retains the active mode. Synchronous `request` refuses a guarded replacement; synchronous release remains available to completed owner cleanup. Allocation and irrigation entry points wait for footprint approval before creating their sessions. <!-- NEW -->

The plugin is enabled in fresh profiles and added once to existing desktop profiles. A later deliberate removal is preserved. `scripts/update-structures-runtime.mjs` synchronizes only its registrations in the shipped bundles. <!-- NEW -->
